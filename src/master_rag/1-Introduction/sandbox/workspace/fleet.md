# Electric Vehicle Fleet Comparison: Tesla Model Y vs Ford Mustang Mach-E

## Executive Summary
For our 100-car sales fleet, we need vehicles that offer optimal range, low total cost of ownership, reliable performance, and charging convenience. This comparison evaluates the Tesla Model Y and Ford Mustang Mach-E as primary candidates.

## Quick Comparison Table

| Feature | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|
| Starting Price | $46,380 | $37,795 (base) - $57,690 (Premium) |
| EPA Range | 294-357 miles | 320 miles (Premium) |
| 0-60 mph | ~140 mph | 3.3 seconds (GT Performance) |
| 5-Year Maintenance | $3,065 | $65,533-$72,882 (5-year total) |
| Warranty | 8-year/100,000-mile powertrain | 3-year/36,000-mile basic, 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain |
| Charging Speed | 182 miles in 15 minutes (Supercharger) | Up to 150 kW DC fast charging |

## Detailed Analysis

### Tesla Model Y
**Advantages for Fleet Operations:**
- **Efficient Charging**: Can add up to 182 miles of range in just 15 minutes using Tesla's extensive supercharger network
- **Lower Maintenance Costs**: Only $3,065 over 5 years ($613 annually), nearly $800 less than comparable gas vehicles
- **Strong Warranty**: 8-year/100,000-mile powertrain and battery warranty provides excellent long-term reliability
- **Performance**: 326 hp with quick acceleration suitable for sales operations

**Considerations:**
- Higher starting price at $46,380
- Requires Tesla's proprietary charging network for optimal performance

### Ford Mustang Mach-E
**Advantages for Fleet Operations:**
- **Competitive Pricing**: Most trims start well below $50,000, with base model at $37,795
- **Plug and Charge**: Built-in feature eliminates need for apps/fobs at compatible stations - ideal for multiple fleet drivers
- **Strong Safety**: 5-star NHTSA overall rating with low rollover risk
- **Fast Charging**: Up to 150 kW DC fast charging capability

**Considerations:**
- Higher 5-year total cost of ownership ($65,533-$72,882)
- Shorter warranty coverage compared to Tesla
- Longer 0-60 mph time in standard models

## Cost Analysis

### Total Cost of Ownership (5 years)
- **Tesla Model Y**: ~$3,065 maintenance + depreciation + charging costs
- **Ford Mustang Mach-E**: $65,533-$72,882 total ownership cost

**Winner**: Tesla Model Y for lower ongoing maintenance costs

### Initial Investment
- **Tesla Model Y**: $46,380 (consistent across trims)
- **Ford Mustang Mach-E**: $37,795-$57,690 (significant variation by trim)

**Winner**: Ford Mustang Mach-E for price flexibility

## Recommendation

**Primary Choice: Ford Mustang Mach-E**

**Rationale:**
1. **Budget-Friendly Entry Point**: The base model at $37,795 fits better with fleet budget constraints
2. **Driver-Friendly Charging**: Plug and Charge feature is superior for multiple drivers in a fleet setting
3. **Safety Compliance**: 5-star NHTSA rating ensures fleet safety standards
4. **Brand Recognition**: Mustang brand appeal may enhance customer confidence in sales interactions

**Secondary Choice: Tesla Model Y**

**Use Case:** Consider Tesla if budget allows and you have drivers who can navigate Tesla's charging ecosystem, plus you prioritize lower long-term maintenance costs.

## Final Decision
For your 100-car sales fleet, I recommend the **Ford Mustang Mach-E** as the primary choice due to its competitive pricing, driver-friendly charging infrastructure, and proven safety record. Allocate budget for approximately 50 base models ($37,795) and 50 Premium models ($57,690) to balance upfront costs with required features.

The Mustang Mach-E's Plug and Charge capability makes it particularly suitable for sales teams where drivers need quick, hassle-free charging between customer visits.
