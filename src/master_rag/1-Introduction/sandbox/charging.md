# Public EV Charging Landscape in the US

## Public Charging Points
- **Total public ports (Jan 2025):** ~195,874 across 69,679 stations (Climate Central).  
- **Growth:** More than 6× increase from ~30,000 in 2016.  
- **State leaders:** California (~49,128 ports), New York (~15,890), Florida (~11,100), Texas (~10,629).

## Major Charging Networks

### Tesla Supercharger
- **Network size:** >33,400 ports nationwide.  
- **Connector:** Proprietary Tesla connector.  
- **Speed:** Up to 250 kW DC fast charging.  
- **Coverage:** Extensive along major highways; continuous expansion.

### Electrify America
- **Network size:** ~5,100 ports.  
- **Connector:** CCS (combined charging system).  
- **Speed:** 150–200 kW DC fast charging.  
- **Coverage:** Rapid growth across the Midwest, Northeast, and West Coast; partnership with major automakers.
