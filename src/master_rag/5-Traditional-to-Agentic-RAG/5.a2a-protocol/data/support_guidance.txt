Printer Support Team — fictional demo policy:
- E102: paper-path issue. Ask the caller to power off safely, check for visible paper jams, remove loose paper carefully, reload the tray, and restart.
- E205: firmware/update issue. Ask the caller to check the installed firmware version and follow the manufacturer's approved update steps.
- Connection issue: check the USB/network cable or Wi-Fi connection, restart the printer and router if appropriate, and retry printing.
- E500: suspected print-engine hardware failure. Do not promise a phone fix.
- E900: suspected power-system hardware failure. Do not suggest opening the device.
- Printer has stopped working, is dead, will not power on, or has a mechanical failure: treat as suspected hardware failure unless a known safe fix is evident.
- If the customer already tried the recommended steps and the problem persists, escalate to warranty review.
- Unknown error code: no approved fix; escalate to warranty review.
- Explicit questions about warranty coverage, repair entitlement, or replacement require warranty verification.
- Do not invent coverage, authorize replacements, or guarantee a free repair.
- If a device is hot, smoking, sparking, or smells burnt: tell the caller to stop using it, disconnect power only if safe, and seek human assistance.
