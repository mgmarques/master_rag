Approved phone guidance (fictional teaching policy):

E102 - Paper jam / paper-path obstruction
  1. Turn the printer off and unplug it.
  2. Open the rear and front covers and gently remove any visible paper.
  3. Check the tray for torn pieces, reload fresh paper, plug in and restart.

E205 - Firmware / update issue
  1. Make sure the printer is connected to the network.
  2. Open the settings menu and run the firmware update.
  3. Do not switch the printer off during the update; restart when it finishes.

Connection failure
  1. Check cables or the Wi-Fi indicator.
  2. Restart the printer and the router.
  3. Re-select the network on the printer and print a test page.

Anything else (E500, E900, dead printer, unknown codes, unresolved issues,
warranty or replacement questions) has NO approved phone fix.
